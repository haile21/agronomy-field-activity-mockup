Six responsibilities → where they live
  • Crop scouting & plant health — Scouting + Plant health sections of the visit form
  • Soil management — Soil sampling section (pH, moisture, N-P-K)
  • Recommending inputs — fertiliser / seed / herbicide / water toggles
  • Management strategy discussion — crop rotation, cultivation, harvest timing
  • Environmental conservation — optional note on the visit form
  • Agricultural extension — "Group extension session" visit type
  • Agricultural research — separate Trials tab
Scope & assumptions
  • Single hardcoded sign-in (Dr. Meseret Tadesse) — no real auth.
  • Farmers list, visit history and trials are seeded placeholder data, dates computed relative to today so the demo always looks current.
  • "Next visit in N days" is a simple offset — no calendar/holiday awareness, no reminder/notification delivery.
  • This mock does not include the AI-confidence/cap ticket flow from AGRONOMY-ADVISORY-SOLUTION-FLOW.html — that's a separate concern (farmer-initiated questions) from this one (agronomist-initiated field visits).
  • No API shapes, entities or endpoints implied — click-through only, for review before anything is built.
